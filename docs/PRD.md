# **PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)**

## **1\. Product Overview**

**Product Name:** Josh Enterprises

**Product Type:** Multi-vendor marketplace platform

**Core Purpose:**  
Josh Enterprises is a marketplace that connects buyers, sellers, service providers, and businesses on one platform. Users can buy products, sell products, offer services, and hire service providers using a single account.

The platform will support product and service listings across all categories and provide integrated communication, payments, escrow, delivery, order management, reviews, disputes, and seller storefronts.

---

# **2\. Product Vision**

To create a trusted marketplace where people and businesses can easily discover, buy, sell, and offer products and services while completing transactions securely within one platform.

# **17\. Escrow System**

Josh Enterprises will use an **escrow-based transaction system**.

### **Basic Flow**

**Buyer pays → Josh Enterprises holds funds → Seller fulfills order → Buyer confirms completion → Funds released to seller.**

The escrow system should:

* Record transaction amount  
* Hold buyer funds  
* Link funds to the order  
* Prevent unauthorized release  
* Release funds after successful completion  
* Keep transaction records  
* Hold funds during disputes

## **40\. Core Product Requirement**

The most important requirement is that **Josh Enterprises must function as a complete marketplace rather than simply a directory of sellers**.

A buyer must be able to discover a product/service, communicate with the seller, make payment, have the transaction protected through escrow, receive the product/service, confirm completion, and review the seller — all within the platform.

Similarly, a seller must be able to register, verify their identity/business, create listings, communicate with buyers, receive and fulfill orders, and receive their funds through the platform.
